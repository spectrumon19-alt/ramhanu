About The Temple

|| श्री अंजनेय स्वामी मंदिर ||

🛕

Sri Anjaneya Swamy Temple

Moosapet, Hyderabad

🙏 A Divine Abode of Lord Hanuman

Sri Anjaneya Swamy Temple, nestled in the heart of Moosapet, Hyderabad, stands as a beacon of devotion, faith, and spiritual strength. This sacred temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman (Anjaneya Swamy), the epitome of devotion, courage, and selfless service.

Lord Hanuman, the ardent devotee of Lord Sri Rama, is revered across India as the remover of obstacles, the bestower of strength, and the protector of devotees. The deity at this temple is believed to be Swayambhu (self-manifested) and carries immense spiritual energy.

The temple attracts thousands of devotees, especially on Tuesdays and Saturdays, which are considered the most auspicious days for Hanuman worship. The divine atmosphere, the rhythmic chanting of Hanuman Chalisa, and the fragrance of sindoor and camphor create an unforgettable spiritual experience.

Festivals & Events

|| उत्सव एवं कार्यक्रम ||

🐒

Hanuman Jayanti

The most grand celebration at the temple. Special abhishekam, 24-hour bhajans, annadanam for thousands of devotees, and magnificent decoration of the deity.

Chaitra Purnima (March/April)
🏹

Ram Navami

Celebration of Lord Sri Rama's birth with special Sita-Rama Kalyanam, processions, and devotional programs throughout the day.

Chaitra Shukla Navami (March/April)
🪔

Deepavali

Festival of lights celebrated with thousands of diyas, fireworks, special pooja, and prasadam distribution. Temple illuminated beautifully.

Kartik Amavasya (October/November)
🎶

Weekly Bhajans & Annadanam

Every Tuesday & Saturday
